About Me

As you know, This site is developed, owned, and administrated by Vinayak Aggarwal . You can make out already that I am a tech geek. I had a love for technology from the beginning and I am already out venturing all the unknowns in the field. Creating this site (although a specimen project) helped me learn new things and it helped me gain experience.

I’m a recent Class 12 graduate with a deep curiosity for how things work—and a growing passion for engineering and electronics.
I’m preparing to start my BTech, and in the meantime:
  • I’ve been tinkering with Arduino—building small projects like a temperature‑based fan controller and a door alert system using sensors, LEDs, relays, and motors. I love seeing hardware respond to code.
  • I designed and launched my own portfolio website using HTML & CSS, where I showcase these Arduino builds and share what I’ve learned along the way.
  • I’m naturally curious and self‑motivated. I learn by doing—whether it’s soldering a circuit or structuring a webpage. I research, test, and refine until it works right.
Why I’m Here

I’m excited to connect with other makers, mentors, and fellow students interested in embedded systems, IoT, and electronics. I’m eager to collaborate on projects, join hackathons, explore internships, or simply exchange ideas.

Let’s Connect

I believe hands‑on learning never stops—and I’d love to support others on their tech journey, too. Let’s share experiences and help each other grow.
